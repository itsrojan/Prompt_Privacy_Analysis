Instructions for the Annotation Task (Click to expand)

To begin the annotation, please follow the instructions below.

  • You will see a ChatGPT prompt–response pair.
  • Read every sentence in the Prompt first, then read every sentence in the Response.
  • To annotate personal information (see "Personal Information Examples" for definition):
    • Highlight the relevant word or phrase that constitutes personal information.
    • Press the corresponding keys for the correct personal information category.(see "Key Instruction" for more details).
      • Press "e" to annotate the highlighted text as "explicit".
      • Press "i" to annotate the highlighted text as "implicit".
      • Press "s" to annotate the highlighted text as "suggested".
      • Press "t" to annotate the highlighted text as "third-party".
    • In the "Type of personal information:" text box, enter the appropriate information type (see "Personal Information Examples").
  • Continue annotating all instances of personal information in both Prompt and the Response.
  • The "History of annotated personal information types:" list will fill automatically as you annotate.
  • When you have finished annotating both the prompt and the response, click Submit Task.
  • No annotations?
    • If the prompt contains no personal information to annotate, tick the checkbox “The prompt does not contain any information to annotate.”
    • If the response contains no personal information to annotate, tick the checkbox “The response does not contain any information to annotate.”

NOTE: Do not overlap annotations. If you need to annotate an instance that is contained within another instance you want to annotate, annotate the longer instance first to avoid duplication.

Personal Information Examples (Click to expand)

Personal information is defined as any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, also known as a "data subject". This means it encompasses any information that, on its own or when combined with other data, can be used to identify a specific individual.

Here are some examples of the personal information you might see in the prompt-response pair. Each personal information instance can belong to a specific category. Additional examples of personal information instances and categories are provided below:

Key Instruction (Click to expand)

There are four personal information categories you'll encounter: explicit, implicit, and suggested. Each category has its own keyboard shortcut (key). The definitions, shortcuts, and examples are below.

Explicit

Explicit category refers to personal information that is stated directly in the text. To annotate, highlight the personal information instance and press "e".
For example: In sentence "The rent amount is $1200 per month.", the highlighted phrase is "explicit" category. Its personal information type is "rent amount per month".

Implicit

Implicit category refers to personal infromation not stated outright but inferable from context. To annotate, highlight the personal information instance and press "i".
For example: In sentence "The rent amount is $1200 per month.", the highlighted word reveals the person's living situation (renting vs owning). "rent" is therefore an "implicit" category. Its personal information type is "living situation".

Suggested

Suggested category refers to personal information (explicit or implicit) that ChatGPT recommends or suggests in its response. To annotate, highlight the personal information instance and press "s".
For example: In sentence "For a memorable dinner, you might like Odd Duck or Lenoir - they are both well-known.", the highlighted words are "suggested" categories. Their personal information type are "activity location" for both.

Third-party

Third-party category refers to personal information (explicit) of any natural or legal person, public authority, agency, or body other than the data subject, controller, or processor, and those acting under their direct authority. Essentially, it's anyone who isn't directly involved in the core processing of personal data but might still have a role or access to it. To annotate, highlight the personal information instance and press "t".
For example: In sentence "My nephew Cam is 19-years-old.", the highlighted words are "third-party" categories. Their personal information type are "name" and "age" respectively.
